# Evaluation matrix

Evaluation of all longlist items (`docs/brainstorm/longlist.md`) against the criteria in
`docs/review/criteria.md`.

- Scores are **expert estimates of the exploratory phase**, made without literature or
  data review. Data-availability scores (`C2`) express expectations — all sources are
  to be verified. Scores should be revisited after the research phase.
- Water flow / flooding is evaluated as **one model with scenarios** (`WAT`), combining
  HYD-01, HYD-02, HYD-03, URB-02, HYD-04 and LND-03 (author decision, 2026-10-12).
  32 items are evaluated in total.
- All criteria: 5 = best for the project (`C4`, `C5`: 5 = simple / cheap).
- Weighted score = Σ (weight × score) / 100. Weights: `C1` 20, `C2` 15, `C3` 15, all other 10.
- ⚠ = knock-out flag (score 1 in `C1` or `C2`).

Only scores of 1 and 5 are justified here; 2–4 follow the scale descriptions directly.

| ID | Criterion | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|

| HAZ-01 | C3, C8 | 5 | Moving fire front, wind/slope effects and "ignite here" interaction are immediately readable; topical in CZ. |
| HAZ-01 | C7 | 5 | Stochastic spread, wind influence and DEM derivatives are shared with many other models; hub of chains L1, L2, L9, L11. |
| WAT | C3, C8 | 5 | Flowing and accumulating water on real terrain is intuitive and relatable (floods in CZ). |
